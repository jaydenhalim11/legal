1999 Studios Uploader – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

1999 Studios Uploader ("the app") is an internal tool made by 1999 Studios, a small group of friends who make Roblox games. We use it to upload videos of our own games to our own TikTok account's drafts. It is not offered to the public.

What the app collects

When we connect our TikTok account, TikTok gives the app:

  • Basic profile info for that account (open ID, display name, avatar), through the user.info.basic scope.
  • Access and refresh tokens that let the app upload videos to that account's drafts, through the video.upload scope.

The app does not collect information about any other TikTok users, viewers, or followers.

How it is used

  • Tokens are used only to upload our studio's own gameplay videos to our TikTok drafts. We review and post every video ourselves inside the TikTok app.
  • Profile info is used only to confirm which account is connected.

Where it is stored

Everything is stored locally, on the computer of the studio member who runs the app. Login tokens are kept in a file that only that computer's user account can read. We do not run a server or database, and we don't store this data anywhere else.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share TikTok data with anyone. The only services the app talks to are:

  • TikTok, to log in and upload our videos.
  • Anthropic (Claude), which looks at still frames from our own gameplay recordings to help pick clips and write captions. No TikTok account data is sent to Anthropic.

Deleting data

We can disconnect the app at any time by deleting the local token file, or by removing the app's access in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions.

Children

The app is only used by 1999 Studios to manage our own account. It does not collect data from anyone else, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
