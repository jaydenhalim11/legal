1999 Studios Uploader – Terms of Service

Effective date: November 17, 2026

What the app is

1999 Studios Uploader ("the app") is an internal tool made by 1999 Studios. It uploads videos of our own Roblox games to our own TikTok account as drafts. It is not offered to the public or to other TikTok users.

Who can use it

Only members of 1999 Studios, with the studio's own TikTok account. By connecting a TikTok account, the user confirms they own or are allowed to manage that account.

How it works

  • The app uploads videos to the connected account's TikTok drafts (inbox). It never posts publicly by itself. A person reviews every draft and posts it in the TikTok app.
  • Only content made by 1999 Studios is uploaded: gameplay and behind-the-scenes footage of our own games.

Rules

Everyone using the app must follow TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. The app must not be used to upload content we don't have the rights to, spam, misleading content, or fake giveaways.

TikTok data

How we handle data from TikTok is described in our Privacy Policy.

Disconnecting

The connected account can remove the app's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions.
